Warsaw & London - November 2019

 Warsaw was a mystery Holiday booked through wowcher.com in september 2019. 

The idea being you pay £99 and a randomly selected holiday/trip is chosen for you. This could be anywhere from Barbados or Malta to barcelona or in our case Warsaw. 

Warsaw is somewhere that, If i had a list of places I wanted to go to, It is  somewhere I probably would have never considered. However I had one of the best times being away with my best friend to a county I had never given a chance, all for £99 (excluding extra ryanair fees).


We Flew To Warsaw from London Stansted Airport after a 7 hour wait at the airport. Where sleep is not permitted.
This was a mistake as it was the latest coach we could get to the airport and I can say I have never gotten a coach since.

The flight was ok, Just not a fan of ryanair since they charged us an extra 64 to bring out bags with us as they "Have wheels".
